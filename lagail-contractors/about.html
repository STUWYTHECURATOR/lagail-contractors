About Lagail Contractors

Solution to Your Problems — Technical, Building & Maintenance Solutions in Zambia

Your Trusted Partner in Engineering & Maintenance

Led by Managing Director Lazarous Phiri, Lagail Contractors (P. O. Box 34935, Lusaka - Zambia) is a Zambian contracting firm specialised in Plumbing, Electrical & Refrigeration, Carpentry & Joinery, Building, Painting & General Maintenance.
